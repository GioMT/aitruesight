Guide 1: Zapier

Lead Capture & CRM Routing

Setup a pipeline linking Google Forms responses directly to HubSpot CRM and sending instant team notifications on Slack.

Prerequisites Checklist

A live Google Form with at least one mock response submitted
Free account registered on Zapier
Free HubSpot developer account initialized
Slack workspace ready with a `#sales-leads` channel
Step 1 of 9

Create the Zap

Log into your Zapier dashboard. Click the black **Create** button in the top-left corner, and select **New Zap**.

1. Trigger: Google Forms
New Response in Spreadsheet
App
2. Action: HubSpot
Create or Update Contact
App
3. Action: Slack
Send Channel Message
App
Real-World Verification Tips

Google Forms Webhook: Make sure your Google Form is linked to a Google Sheet. It's often easiest to trigger from Google Sheets using "New Spreadsheet Row" if your form data requires custom pre-processing or spreadsheet formula calculations.
HubSpot Scopes: When connecting Zapier to HubSpot, make sure to grant read/write permissions for contacts. The default OAuth pop-up will handle this, but verify you are using an admin or developer sandbox user.

Guide 2: Make.com

Multi-Platform Cross-Posting

Automatically sync social media queue rows from a Google Sheet and push them out to LinkedIn and Twitter simultaneously using a Router.

Prerequisites Checklist

Google Sheet with columns: `Post Title`, `Content`, and `Status`
Add one mock post row and set `Status` to `Ready`
Free account registered on Make.com
LinkedIn and Twitter (X) profiles ready for connection
Step 1 of 9

Initialize the Scenario

Log into Make. Click the purple **Create a new scenario** button in the top-right corner. Click the large, pulsing circle with a **+** sign in the center.

Real-World Verification Tips

Twitter/X Free Tier Rates: X's free basic API tier has very tight rate limits (currently restricted to ~17 automated tweets per day). During testing, limit your automated post executions to prevent temporary endpoint blocking.
Initial State Sync: In Make, when running a scenario for the first time, Make will ask where you'd like to start reading rows. Choose "All" or select a specific row ID for testing to verify it handles old row data correctly.

Guide 3: Make.com

Weekly KPI Reporting Digests

Schedule a recurring cron scenario that reads a cell coordinate from your sheets dashboard and prints a formatted digest message in Slack.

Prerequisites Checklist

Google Sheet containing a calculated metric summary (e.g. cell `B2`)
Make.com and Slack accounts signed in
Step 1 of 6

Set the Schedule

Create a fresh scenario in Make. Click the tiny clock icon attached to the default blank module circle. In the **Run scenario** dropdown, switch it to **Days of the week**. Check **Monday**, type `09:00` into the time input fields, and click **OK**.

Real-World Verification Tips

Formula Output Sync: If the target cell (e.g. `B2`) contains spreadsheet calculations, Make's Google Sheets module retrieves the calculated output value. Make sure you check "Value Render Option: Formatted Value" in the module setup so that symbols like `$` or `%` are cleanly pushed to Slack without losing formatting.

Guide 4: Zapier

E-commerce Order Notifications

Connect Shopify triggers with Twilio SMS dispatch, routing customers' phones through a Formatter logic interceptor to structure numbers correctly.

Prerequisites Checklist

Shopify store login or a developer sandbox store access
Twilio developer account with an active virtual phone number
Zapier account signed in
Step 1 of 7

Initialize Shopify

Create a new Zap in your Zapier dashboard. Choose **Shopify** for your trigger step. Set the **Event** dropdown to **New Order** and hit **Continue**. Sign in by entering your primary `.myshopify.com` store URL string.

1. Trigger: Shopify
New Order
App
2. Helper: Formatter
Format Phone Number
Logic
3. Action: Twilio
Send SMS
App
Real-World Verification Tips

Twilio Trial Accounts: If you are testing this using a free Twilio developer account, Twilio will only send SMS messages to phone numbers that have been manually added and verified inside your Twilio Sandbox Console. Ensure you verify your personal cell number there first.
Empty Phone Safeguard: If a Shopify customer checks out without inputting a phone number, the Formatter step will receive a blank/null value and fail. In the real world, add a "Filter by Zapier" step right after Shopify to only continue if the customer phone number field is present.
